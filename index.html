How a preview works

Built from the creator's videos, kept for the creator

  • Written from your own words

    Articles are adapted from the transcript of the creator's own videos, so the ideas and the voice are theirs.

  • Every section links to the moment in the video

    A reader can jump from any section straight to the point in the video where it was said.

  • Hidden until claimed

    Previews are kept out of search engines, carry no ads and are not used by Galascribe in any other way.

  • Yours to keep

    Claiming a preview moves the site to the creator's own GitHub account.
